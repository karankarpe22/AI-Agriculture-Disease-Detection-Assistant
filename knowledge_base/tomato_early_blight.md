# Tomato Early Blight Management

- **Title**: Integrated Management of Early Blight in Tomato
- **Source**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR), Varanasi & MPKV Rahuri
- **Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Disease**: Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)
- **Reference**: ICAR Package of Practices for Vegetable Crops

## Symptoms
Initial infection begins on older, low-lying leaves as dark brown to black circular or irregular necrotic spots. As the lesions expand, characteristic concentric rings develop resembling a miniature bullseye or target pattern, frequently surrounded by a chlorotic yellow zone. Progressive infection causes collar rot at the stem base and dark sunken leathery lesions at the fruit stem attachment end. Infected leaves dry up and shed prematurely.

## Favorable Environmental Conditions
Moderate to warm temperatures (24°C to 29°C) combined with alternating periods of high humidity (heavy dew or rain) and sunny dry intervals. Stressed, senescent, or poorly fertilized plants are especially prone to infection.

## Cultural Practices & IPM
1. Crop rotation: Never follow tomato immediately after potato, eggplant, or capsicum. Rotate with corn, okra, or pulses for 2 years.
2. Staking and mulching: Staking plants off the ground and applying black mulch prevents soil-borne fungal spores from splashing onto lower leaves.
3. Pruning lower leaves: Remove bottom foliage up to 20 cm from the soil once fruit begins setting to minimize soil-leaf contact.
4. Balanced nutrition: Avoid nitrogen deficiency; top-dress with balanced potassium to fortify plant cell wall defenses.

## Recommended Fungicides & Application Schedule
1. **Preventive Stage**:
   - Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L, OR Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L, applied every 10–14 days.
2. **Curative Stage (When lesions are detected)**:
   - Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml/L, OR
   - Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L, OR
   - Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1.0–1.2 ml/L, OR
   - Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1.0 g/L.

## Safety & Waiting Period
Always alternate strobilurin fungicides (FRAC 11) with triazole fungicides (FRAC 3) to prevent fungal resistance. Observe a 3–5 day pre-harvest interval before fruit collection.
