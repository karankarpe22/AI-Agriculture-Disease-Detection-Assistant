# Potato Early Blight Management

- **Title**: Diagnosis and Management of Early Blight in Potato
- **Source**: ICAR - Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI), Shimla
- **Crop**: Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*)
- **Disease**: Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)
- **Reference**: ICAR-CPRI Technical Bulletin No. 92, Potato Pathology Guide

## Symptoms
Initial symptoms appear on older lower leaves as small, dry, brown to dark brown necrotic spots. Over time, these spots enlarge and exhibit characteristic concentric rings or "target board" (target spot) patterns bounded by leaf veins. Surrounding leaf tissue typically turns chlorotic (yellow). Under severe infestation, the foliage dries up, droops, and premature defoliation occurs, drastically reducing tuber yield and size.

## Favorable Environmental Conditions
Alternating wet and dry periods with temperatures ranging between 22°C and 28°C and relative humidity around 80% favor pathogen sporulation and infection. Stressed or nitrogen-deficient crops are especially susceptible.

## Cultural & Preventative Practices
1. Plant certified, disease-free seed tubers.
2. Maintain vigorous crop growth through balanced nitrogen and potassium fertilization. Avoid excess moisture stress.
3. Crop rotation with non-host crops (maize, sorghum, sugarcane) for at least 2 seasons.
4. Destruction of crop debris and infected haulms immediately after tuber harvest.
5. Provide furrow irrigation or drip irrigation; avoid overhead sprinkler wetting of foliage late in the day.

## Recommended Chemical & Fungicidal Management
1. Prophylactic protective spray: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0–2.5 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L.
2. Curative systemic spray (upon symptom manifestation):
   - Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L, OR
   - Tebuconazole 50% + Trifloxystrobin 25% WG @ 0.6–0.7 g/L, OR
   - Mancozeb + Metalaxyl @ 2.5 g/L.
3. Repeat spray after 10–12 days if warm and humid weather persists.

## Precautions
Rotate fungicides from different chemical groups (FRAC codes) to prevent fungicide resistance in *Alternaria solani*.
