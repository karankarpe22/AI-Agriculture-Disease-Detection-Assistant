# Good Agricultural Practices for Healthy Tomato Cultivation

- **Title**: Integrated Crop Management and Preventative Practices for Healthy Tomato Production
- **Source**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR), Varanasi & TNAU Agritech
- **Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Topic**: Healthy Plant Canopy, Nutrition, and Soil Health
- **Reference**: ICAR National Guideline for Protected and Open Field Tomato Production

## Visual Indicators of Healthy Tomato Canopy
Healthy tomato foliage has robust, dark green leaves without chlorotic mottling, necrosis, curled margins, or stippling. Leaf petioles form strong angular attachments to the central stem. Flowering clusters have bright yellow, well-formed petals, and fruit sets develop uniform green coloration without blossom-end browning.

## Soil, Land Preparation & Nutrition
1. Optimum soil: Well-aerated sandy loam with pH 6.0–6.8 and good organic matter content.
2. Basal fertilization: Apply 25 tonnes/ha well-rotted Farmyard Manure (FYM) fortified with *Trichoderma viride* (2.5 kg/ha) and *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (2.5 kg/ha) 15 days prior to planting.
3. Mineral nutrition: N:P:K 150:100:100 kg/ha with nitrogen applied in 3 equal splits (at transplanting, 30 days, and 60 days).
4. Micronutrient spray: Apply foliar spray of vegetable micronutrient mixture (containing Zn, B, Fe, Mn) @ 2.5 g/L at flower initiation and fruit enlargement.

## Irrigation & Microclimate Management
1. Regulate moisture evenly using drip irrigation to avoid fruit cracking and blossom-end rot (caused by erratic water supply and localized calcium deficiency).
2. Staking: Stake indeterminate and semi-determinate tomato varieties using bamboo poles and plastic twine to keep foliage 30 cm above soil level.
3. Aeration: Prune lower auxiliary shoots (suckers) up to the first flower cluster to maintain canopy air circulation and sunlight penetration.

## Preventative IPM Measures
1. Install yellow sticky traps (10–12 traps/acre) for whiteflies and pheromone traps (5 traps/acre) for tomato fruit borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*).
2. Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or 0.3% Neem oil (3 ml/L) as a prophylactic pest deterrent every 15 days.
3. Monitor the field twice a week for early signs of disease or nutrient deficiency.
