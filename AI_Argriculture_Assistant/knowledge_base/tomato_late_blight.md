# Tomato Late Blight Management

- **Title**: Management Strategy for Late Blight in Tomato
- **Source**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR) & Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI)
- **Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Disease**: Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)
- **Reference**: ICAR Plant Protection Bulletin: Oomycete Disease Management

## Symptoms
Large, irregular, pale-green to water-soaked dark brown or purplish-black lesions appear abruptly on leaves and petioles. Under cool, humid mornings, a delicate downy white mold (sporulation) appears on the abaxial (lower) surface of infected leaves along the lesion perimeter. Stems develop dark brown greasy lesions leading to branch collapse. Developing green fruits display greasy, olive-brown to bronze, firm water-soaked lesions that cause fruit rot.

## Favorable Environmental Conditions
Cool temperatures (15°C to 22°C) with persistent high relative humidity (>90%), dense fog, continuous drizzle, or sustained dew. The disease spreads extremely rapidly via airborne sporangia and can ruin an entire tomato patch within 5 to 7 days.

## Preventative Cultural Measures
1. Avoid planting adjacent to potato fields infected with late blight.
2. Space rows generously (at least 90 cm) to maximize air movement through the foliage.
3. Eliminate wild solanaceous weeds (*Solanum nigrum*) around field perimeters.
4. Adopt drip irrigation; do not use overhead irrigation during cool cloudy weather.

## Recommended Fungicidal Management
1. **Preventive (Before disease onset when cool wet weather is forecasted)**:
   - Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L, OR
   - Propineb 70% WP @ 2.5 g/L, OR
   - Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L.
2. **Curative / Systemic (At first appearance of water-soaked spots)**:
   - Metalaxyl-M 4% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2.5 g/L, OR
   - Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 3.0 g/L, OR
   - Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L combined with Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L, OR
   - Famoxadone 16.6% + Cymoxanil 22.1% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.
3. Repeat spray at 7-day intervals under active disease pressure.

## Critical Precautions
Do not apply the same systemic fungicide more than twice in succession to forestall fungicide resistance development.
