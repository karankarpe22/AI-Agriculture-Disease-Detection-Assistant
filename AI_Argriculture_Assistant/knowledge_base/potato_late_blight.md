# Potato Late Blight Management

- **Title**: Integrated Management of Late Blight of Potato
- **Source**: ICAR - Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI), Shimla
- **Crop**: Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*)
- **Disease**: Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)
- **Reference**: CPRI Advisory on Potato Blight Forecasting and Management

## Symptoms
Water-soaked, irregular, light-green to pale spots develop on the tips and margins of lower leaves. In cool, humid weather, these rapidly enlarge into large, dark-brown or purplish-black necrotic lesions. A distinct delicate white mildew (cottony fungal growth/sporangiophores) develops on the lower surface of the leaf encircling the lesion margins, particularly in early morning dew. The disease quickly travels down stems and petioles causing vine collapse with a foul rotting odor.

## Favorable Environmental Conditions
Cool temperatures (12°C to 20°C) with prolonged high relative humidity (>90%) and continuous cloud cover, fog, or intermittent drizzle create epidemic conditions. The disease can decimate an entire field within 7 to 10 days if left untreated.

## Preventative & Cultural Control
1. Plant certified resistant or tolerant potato varieties (e.g., Kufri Girdhari, Kufri Khyati, Kufri Mohan where adapted).
2. Use healthy seed tubers from certified disease-free cold storage sources.
3. Prophylactic earthing-up to prevent sporangia from washing down into the tuber zone.
4. Haulm cutting (dehaulming) 10–12 days prior to harvesting to prevent tuber infection during digging.

## Chemical Control Schedule (CPRI Strategy)
1. **Prophylactic (Preventive) Phase**:
   - Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Propineb 70% WP @ 2.5 g/L as soon as weather forecast predicts cool, overcast, and humid conditions before any disease appears.
2. **Curative / Systemic Phase (When first blight lesions appear)**:
   - Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 3.0 g/L, OR
   - Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L tank-mixed with Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L, OR
   - Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil MZ) @ 2.5 g/L, OR
   - Mandipropamid 23.4% SC @ 0.8 ml/L.
3. Ensure thorough coverage of both upper and lower leaf canopy surfaces. Reapply after 7–10 days if humid conditions persist.

## Safety & Post-Harvest Precautions
Do not harvest tubers from fields where live blight remains on foliage. Sort and discard any blighted tubers before cold storage to avoid secondary soft rot.
